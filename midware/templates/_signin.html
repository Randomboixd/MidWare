OpenCode Go usage in MidWare may be against the terms of service.

Depending on your usage, connecting OpenCode Go to your MidWare may not be a good idea.

OpenCode Go is a plan designed for agentic usage: the OpenCode app, and other agentic apps such as Hermes Agent. OpenCode lists harnesses like Hermes as supported clients and asks every client to send a stable session id, so MidWare sends one on every request. Go rejects the request outright without it, and the same header is what keeps prompt caching working.

What is not promised: the plan is sold for coding traffic, Go says traffic is monitored for abuse that degrades the experience for other users, and the terms reserve the right to suspend an account at their discretion. There is no published rule against other kinds of use, and no promise that other kinds of use are safe.

MidWare identifies itself as MidWare/{{ app_version }} when a client sends no user agent of its own. It never impersonates OpenCode, and it cannot reach the free models, which only the OpenCode client can use.
