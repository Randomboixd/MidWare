How scoring works

Each probe scores 0-100: it starts at 100 and subtracts for slow first tokens, slow totals, stray non-Latin symbols and configured slop words. Touch or hover a chart point to see the exact deductions. A recorder stays under examination until it has 24 hours of history, then turns Working or Degraded from its recent average.

Recorders with hardcore checks enabled can turn Failing immediately: a truncated stream, malformed chunk, leaked template token, repetition loop or missed long-context needle is not a statistical question. Enable them per recorder under the form’s Advanced section.
